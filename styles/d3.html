Style 4 - Slide from Right

Use the button to switch between mobile and desktop preview.

A clean and convenient navigation pattern designed for slide-in mobile navigation. On smaller screens, the standard navigation transforms into a hamburger menu that opens a vertical panel from the right side, providing quick access to navigation links while keeping the interface simple and organized.

The menu closes automatically when you tap outside the panel, tap any link, press the close button, scroll the page, or use the ESC key. It also supports a natural gesture — you can close it with a right‑swipe, which feels intuitive on mobile devices.
